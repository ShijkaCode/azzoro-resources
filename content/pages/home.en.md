Azzoro Resources is an ASX-listed explorer focused on copper, nickel, gold and other critical metals in Mongolia. The portfolio is led by the Oval Cu·Ni·PGE discovery and supported by a pipeline of earlier-stage opportunities across the country.

The homepage is designed to give investors a quick read on the discovery case, project pipeline, sustainability approach and leadership depth, with direct paths through to the project pages and external investor centre.
