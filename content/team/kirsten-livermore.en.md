Kirsten Livermore has over 25 years' experience in mining-related policy, regulation and issue management. She served for 15 years in the Australian Parliament, representing a large rural electorate with a significant resources sector. Kirsten led the Australia–Mongolia Extractives Program, working closely with the Mongolian Government and partners to improve mining law, regulation, geoscience data and professional standards. She holds a law degree from the University of Queensland and an MSc in Development Management from the London School of Economics.
