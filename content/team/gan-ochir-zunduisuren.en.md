Gan-Ochir has over 20 years of experience across the mining industry, spanning operations, mineral exploration and finance. He has served as a Non-Executive Director of ASX-listed Aspire Mining Ltd and of Oyu Tolgoi LLC. He studied mining at the Haileybury School of Mines in Canada and the Mongolian University of Science and Technology, holds an MSc in Finance from New York University, and is a Member of AusIMM.
