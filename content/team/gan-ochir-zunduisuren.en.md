Gan-Ochir has over 20 years of experience across a wide range of areas within the mining industry, including mining operations, mineral exploration and finance. He has held Non-Executive Director roles with ASX-listed Aspire Mining Ltd and with Oyu Tolgoi LLC. He obtained his mining education from the Haileybury School of Mines, Canada and the Mongolian University of Science and Technology, holds an MSc in Finance from New York University, and is a Member of AusIMM.
