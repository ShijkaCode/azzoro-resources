David Paull has over 30 years' experience in the mineral resources sector. Over the past decade he has worked in Mongolia with ASX-listed Aspire Mining Ltd in roles including Managing Director and Chairman of the Board. He holds a Bachelor of Commerce from the University of Western Australia, an MBA with Distinction from Cornell University, is a Fellow of the Financial Services Institute of Australia, and previously held a marketing management role at the Greenbushes lithium and tantalum mine.
