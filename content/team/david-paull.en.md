David Paull has over 30 years' experience in the mineral resource business. Over the last decade he has worked in Mongolia with ASX-listed Aspire Mining Ltd in various capacities, including Managing Director and Chairman of the Board. He holds a Bachelor of Commerce from the University of Western Australia and an MBA with Distinction from Cornell University, and is a fellow of the Financial Services Institute of Australia. He previously held a marketing management position at the Greenbushes lithium and tantalum mine.
