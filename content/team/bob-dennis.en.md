Executive Consultant, Geology and Mining — BSc (Hons, First Class), MAIG. Bob brings broad experience across mineral exploration, feasibility, due diligence, resource audits, geologic reviews, mine geology, and mining and metallurgical management, gathered over 40 years in the industry. He has worked on an extensive range of commodities and deposit styles, including copper, lead, zinc, silver, gold, cobalt, iron ore, manganese, tin, tungsten, zircon, molybdenum, graphite and lithium.
