Neil Young is Chief Executive Officer of Elixir Energy Ltd and has more than 20 years' experience in senior management roles across the upstream and downstream energy sector, including with EY, Tarong Energy and Santos. He has developed new ventures in Kazakhstan, Japan, the USA and Mongolia. He holds an M.A. (Hons) joint degree in Economics and Politics from the University of Edinburgh.
