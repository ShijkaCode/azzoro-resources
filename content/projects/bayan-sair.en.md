## Overview

Bayan Sair is an early-stage regional exploration tenement secured by government tender in 2025, immediately south of the Yambat Project. The ground covers a prominent regional magnetic anomaly interpreted as a potential extension of the MS1 and Oval intrusive zones, making it a logical next step as the Company tests how far the Yambat corridor may continue.

## Geology and exploration

The tenement is largely covered by Quaternary sediments, with conceptual targets including mafic intrusion-related and conductive sulphide zones adjacent to the Oval and MS1 intrusions. Regional aeromagnetic data, including the public MRPAM dataset, has been reprocessed by Southern Geoscience Consultants to support target generation. Initial drill-testing and ground electromagnetic surveys have commenced, with interpretation feeding into the Company's broader regional program.
