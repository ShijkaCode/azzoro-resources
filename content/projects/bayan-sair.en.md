## Overview

Bayan Sair is an early-stage regional exploration tenement secured by government tender in 2025, immediately south of the Yambat Project. The ground covers a prominent regional magnetic anomaly interpreted as a potential extension of the MS1 and Oval intrusive zones — making it a priority target as the Company tests its district-scale model.

## Geology and exploration

The tenement is largely covered by Quaternary sediments, with conceptual targets including mafic intrusion-related and conductive sulphide zones, regionally adjacent to the Oval and MS1 intrusions. Regional aeromagnetic data (a public MRPAM dataset) has been reprocessed by Southern Geoscience Consultants to assist target generation. Initial drill-testing and ground electromagnetic surveys have commenced, with interpretation feeding into the Company's broader regional program.
